Final project

ISP Customer Churn Dashboard

Power BI · SQL · Power Query

An ISP tracked installs and dismantles in Excel, and analyzing it took forever. So I built the database with SQL and Power Query, then made a 4-page Power BI dashboard with 7 KPIs on 5,000 rows of customer data, for the Head of Operations and Head of Affco.

Plot twist: Regional 3 had the highest churn even with the lowest installs (468). That’s where retention efforts should start. Also, Tuesday is the busiest install day, over 3x Monday or Sunday.
